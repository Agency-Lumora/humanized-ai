## 🔥 Key Messaging Principles Applied

### 1. **Problem-First Approach**
Every section leads with the problem before offering the solution

### 2. **Customer Language**
Written from customer perspective: "Your website", "Your business", "Your customers"

### 3. **Outcome-Focused**
Focus on results: "Brings in customers", "Stop losing customers", "Actually works"

### 4. **Direct & Clear**
No fluff, no jargon, straight to the point

### 5. **Urgency Without Pressure**
"Costing you customers" creates urgency, but "Let's fix it" feels collaborative
