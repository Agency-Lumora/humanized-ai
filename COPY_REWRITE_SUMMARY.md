# Lumora Copy Rewrite Summary

## Overview
Complete copy rewrite across agencylumora.com to emphasize **client independence**, **transparency**, and **empowerment**. The new messaging is direct, confident, and polished—inspired by insweb.io's clarity and conversion focus.

---

## Key Messaging Changes

### Core Positioning
**Before:** Generic agency buzzwords, vague promises  
**After:** Clear value proposition with emphasis on client ownership

**New Tagline:** "We build your website. Then we teach you how to own it."

### Client Independence Model
- **1 month of FREE support** after launch (clearly communicated)
- Training and teaching included to ensure client confidence
- No dependency tactics—clients own and manage their websites
- After free month, additional support is optional and chargeable
- Positioned as transparency and empowerment, not limitation

## Tone & Voice Guidelines

### What Changed:
- **Removed:** Buzzwords like "revolutionary," "game-changing," "next-generation"
- **Removed:** Vague promises and exaggerated claims
- **Added:** Direct, outcome-focused language
- **Added:** Specific service descriptions
- **Added:** Clear pricing and support model transparency

### New Voice Characteristics:
- **Direct:** No fluff, straight to the point
- **Confident:** We know what we do and do it well
- **Polished:** Professional without being corporate
- **Transparent:** Clear about what happens after launch
- **Empowering:** Focused on client independence and ownership

---

## Key Differentiators Emphasized

1. **Client Independence:** We teach you to manage your own website
2. **1 Month Free Support:** Included training and handoff period
3. **No Dependency Tactics:** You own it, we don't keep you locked in
4. **Full Stack Team:** Everything from one team (no vendor juggling)
5. **Practical Technology:** AI, CRM, SEO only when it solves real problems
6. **Real Results:** Measured by trust, leads, and growth

---

## What Was NOT Changed

- Visual design, animations, and purple accent colors
- Component structure and layout
- Functionality and user interactions
- Existing client testimonials (kept authentic)
- Portfolio case studies (kept real results)
